# beg-boonsville-secondary-gas-recovery

**Covers:** A Gas Research Institute / Bureau of Economic Geology integrated reservoir study (Boonsville field secondary gas recovery project, Fort Worth Basin, Texas), including appendices on Bend Conglomerate/Atoka sequence stratigraphy, reservoir engineering, petrophysics, 3-D seismic acquisition/processing, attributes, and VSP calibration.

**Authoritative for:** Bend Conglomerate (Lower Atoka Group) sequence stratigraphy at Boonsville field: genetic sequences, bounding flooding/erosion surfaces, systems tracts, reservoir compartmentalization styles in Fort Worth Basin Atokan gas reservoirs (valley-fill, facies-, cement-, and fault-bounded; karst-collapse faulting), accommodation-setting controls on sandstone geometry, compartment size, and infill reserve potential, Boonsville-specific stratigraphic nomenclature (Bend Conglomerate vs Atoka Group, Caddo Limestone, Vineyard, Jasper Creek, Wizard Wells sequences), infill/secondary gas recovery evaluation methods: p/z material balance, Fetkovich decline type curves, pressure buildup analysis, RFT pressure data for detecting undrained compartments, net pay and net reservoir cutoff conventions used in the study (SP, resistivity, porosity, Vsh, Sw), log analysis of old ES/IES suites (Vsh from SP, Rt from lateral/long normal, dual-water Sw) and core-calibrated electrofacies modeling, high-frequency 3-D seismic survey design and acquisition practice: wavetesting, array length limits, staggered-line geometry, statics, dense velocity analysis, spectral balancing, complex-trace/instantaneous attributes (amplitude, phase, frequency) as discontinuity indicators, VSP depth-to-time calibration and velocity anisotropy for tying thin beds to a 3-D volume

**Not good for:** basin-scale Fort Worth Basin exploration or Barnett Shale resource assessment (Barnett appears only as source rock; predates the shale-gas play), modern unconventional/horizontal drilling and hydraulic fracture design practice, reservoir characterization outside Pennsylvanian Ouachita-foreland clastics — do not transplant its cutoffs, compartment sizes, or drainage areas to other basins, current seismic acquisition and processing technology (no prestack depth migration, modern broadband or nodal systems, ML workflows), geochemistry, source-rock maturity modeling, or fluid PVT work, water-drive or overpressured reservoir behavior (these reservoirs are underpressured and essentially water-free)

**Scope:** Mid-1990s GRI-funded case study of a ~10 mi2 project area in Boonsville (Bend Conglomerate Gas) field, Wise and Jack Counties, Fort Worth Basin, north-central Texas; Atokan–Strawn Pennsylvanian section ~1,300 ft thick at ~4,000–6,000 ft depth; conclusions on well spacing, economics, and technology reflect 1990s costs, data, and software.
