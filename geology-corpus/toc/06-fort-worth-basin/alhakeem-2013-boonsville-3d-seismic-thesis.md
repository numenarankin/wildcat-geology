# alhakeem-2013-boonsville-3d-seismic-thesis

**Covers:** A 2013 master's thesis interpreting the public Boonsville (BEG/SGR) 3D seismic survey and well-log suite over the Bend Conglomerate (lower Atoka) gas reservoirs of the Fort Worth Basin, Texas, from synthetic seismogram ties and horizon picking through time-depth conversion, isopach/RMS amplitude mapping, petrophysics, and gas-in-place volumetrics.

**Authoritative for:** Boonsville field geology: Bend Conglomerate / lower Atoka reservoir depths, porosity, permeability, pressure, and production history, Details of the Boonsville 3D seismic dataset (acquisition, processing sequence, bin size, sample rate, SEGY headers, survey corner coordinates, well and checkshot/VSP inventory), MFS-numbered formation tops (Caddo, Davis, Runaway, Beans Creek, Vineyard, Wade) and their seismic expression, time structure, and depth maps in the Boonsville survey, Fort Worth Basin regional tectonic framework, bounding structures, stratigraphy, and Barnett Shale–Bend Conglomerate petroleum system as background, Worked seismic-to-well workflow: synthetic seismogram generation, time-depth chart building with sparse checkshots, average and interval velocity calculation, time-to-depth map conversion, isopach construction, Practical petrophysics substitutes: Vsh from SP, Faust velocity-from-resistivity, Gardner density estimation when RHOB is missing, Sw, permeability, Bg, Volumetric ROGIP calculation for Runaway and Vineyard Formations, with assumed recovery factor and average reservoir parameters, Log-based recognition of fluvio-deltaic channel sands, point bars, and karst collapse features tied to seismic

**Not good for:** Barnett Shale unconventional resource assessment, TOC/maturity modeling, or hydraulic fracturing, Reservoirs or basins outside the Fort Worth Basin — parameters, velocities, and Bg values are field-specific, Modern quantitative interpretation methods (prestack inversion, AVO, machine learning attributes); work is post-stack, horizon- and amplitude-based, Rigorous uncertainty quantification or probabilistic reserves — volumetrics are deterministic with an assumed recovery factor, Core-based sedimentology, biostratigraphy, or provenance beyond a summary of prior work, Authoritative stratigraphic nomenclature; it applies the established BEG/Hardage MFS framework rather than defining it

**Scope:** Single-field study: Boonsville field, Wise and Jack Counties, north-central Texas, Fort Worth Basin; Pennsylvanian (Atokan) Bend Conglomerate section above the Mississippian Barnett. Based on the 1990s BEG/Secondary Gas Recovery public 3D survey and its legacy well logs, interpreted with 2013-era commercial software; petrophysical values often derived from proxy relationships where curves were missing, so treat log-derived porosity and Sw as estimates. Unpublished master's thesis, not peer-reviewed.
