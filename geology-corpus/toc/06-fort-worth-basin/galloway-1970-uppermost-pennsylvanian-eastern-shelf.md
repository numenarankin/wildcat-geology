# galloway-1970-uppermost-pennsylvanian-eastern-shelf

**Covers:** Galloway's 1970 report (BEG-style) on depositional systems of the uppermost Pennsylvanian (Harpersville Formation, Cisco Group) on the Eastern Shelf of north-central Texas, with facies models, subsurface isolith maps, cross sections, measured outcrop sections, and locality guides.

**Authoritative for:** Harpersville Formation stratigraphy, subdivision, and limestone marker correlation (Flippen, Breckenridge, Crystal Falls, Saddle Creek) in north-central Texas, Cisco Group fluvial-deltaic depositional systems: channel, point bar, abandoned-channel/clay plug, overbank, and delta lobe complexes and their recognition criteria in outcrop, core, and SP logs, Interdeltaic embayment facies of the Cisco: strike-fed strandplain/shoreface sheet sandstones (incl. Spy Mountain Sandstone), coal/lignite, chamosite, mounded limestones, Sweetwater slope system: shelf-margin sandstones, slope troughs, slope wedges, mudstone and limestone aprons, plus Sylvester shelf-edge carbonate banks, Eastern Shelf progradation into the Midland Basin, shelf-margin to basin-floor relief, and sequential shelf construction, Argument for intrabasinal (deltaic/tectonic) rather than eustatic control of Late Pennsylvanian cyclicity on the Eastern Shelf, Localized Cisco oil production controls, reservoir facies, likely source beds, and structural hinges in Shackelford/Callahan counties, Detailed bed-by-bed measured sections and named outcrop localities in Eastland, Stephens, Young, and Jack counties for field trip or analog work, Methods for net-sandstone isolith mapping from SP/electrical logs and dip cross-section construction, Well lists (Appendix III) for the published cross sections

**Not good for:** Modern sequence-stratigraphic or chronostratigraphic frameworks; predates sequence stratigraphy and modern conodont/isotope calibration, and explicitly downplays eustatic cyclostratigraphy now widely accepted for Late Paleozoic icehouse cycles, Quantitative petrophysics: no porosity/permeability, saturation, or log-calculation workflows; sandstone mapping relies on visual SP estimation, Reservoir engineering, production volumes, or post-1970 field development, unconventional/Barnett Shale evaluation, Other basins or areas: Midland Basin interior, Delaware Basin, Fort Worth Basin proper, Ouachita/Arbuckle source terranes are only peripheral context, Older or younger stratigraphy (Strawn, Canyon lower Cisco detail, Permian Wolfcampian shelf) except as bounding context, Geochemistry, biostratigraphic taxonomy, or absolute ages, Carbonate diagenesis and porosity evolution of the shelf-edge banks

**Scope:** Late Pennsylvanian (Virgilian, uppermost Cisco Group) of the Eastern Shelf, north-central Texas, roughly Eastland tThrough Stephens, Young, Jack, Shackelford, Callahan counties, based on outcrop measured sections plus several hundred pre-1970 electric logs extending westward into the shallow subsurface toward the Midland Basin. Interpretations, terminology, and analogs (La Jolla, Astoria fans; Mississippi and Guadalupe deltas) reflect ca. 1970 process sedimentology; treat correlations, well lists, and cyclicity conclusions as historical but the facies descriptions and outcrop data as durable primary observation.
