# wermund-1975-upper-penn-limestone-banks

**Covers:** A 1975 report by Wermund on Upper Pennsylvanian (Missourian–Wolfcampian) limestone banks of North-Central Texas, combining subsurface isolith/trend-surface mapping on the Eastern shelf with outcrop facies description of the Possum Kingdom Bank.

**Authoritative for:** geometry, thickness, and areal extent of the Palo Pinto and Winchell limestone banks in the North-Central Texas subsurface, carbonate bank vs reef, biostrome vs bioherm terminology as applied to Pennsylvanian shelf carbonates, phylloid algal (Eugonophyllum, Archaeolithophyllum) bank facies, allochem assemblages, and osagiid grainstones, facies succession and mud-mound relief within the Possum Kingdom Bank, Palo Pinto County outcrops, noncalcitic mineral content (terrigenous quartz, clay, chert) of Eastern shelf bank limestones, shelf-edge versus basinward slope limestone character from well cuttings, Eastern shelf into Midland basin, comparison to Pennsylvanian algal mound analogs in Kansas and Oklahoma and modern Florida Bay/Bahamas settings, interplay of deltaic progradation and termination of carbonate bank growth

**Not good for:** reservoir petrophysics, log-based porosity/permeability, or production and completion data, modern sequence-stratigraphic or chemostratigraphic frameworks and revised Pennsylvanian nomenclature, carbonate banks outside the Eastern shelf / Fort Worth Basin region of North-Central Texas, diagenesis, burial history, geochemistry, or isotope work, pre-Missourian or post-Wolfcampian stratigraphy, structural or basin-modeling analyses

**Scope:** Upper Pennsylvanian (Missourian, extending into Wolfcampian) strata of the Eastern shelf, Fort Worth Basin and adjacent Midland basin margin, North-Central Texas; 1975 vintage, so descriptive facies and mapping work using pre-modern nomenclature and cuttings-based subsurface interpretation.
