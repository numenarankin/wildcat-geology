# drillingmanual-cement-bond-log

**Covers:** A drilling-manual chapter section on cement integrity evaluation, focused on the cement bond log (CBL): amplitude response, bond index calculation, waveform signatures, and interpretive limitations.

**Authoritative for:** reading CBL amplitude logs and distinguishing free pipe, good bond, and partial bond, definition and calculation of cement bond index, rule-of-thumb bonded footage at a given bond index required for zonal isolation, interpreting CBL/VDL waveform character and known CBL pitfalls (microannulus, fast formation, thin cement sheath)

**Not good for:** ultrasonic and radial imaging tools (USIT, CET, segmented bond tools) and azimuthal cement mapping, cement slurry design, additives, thickening time, or compressive strength development, primary or remedial cementing job execution, squeeze design, and displacement mechanics, quantitative log processing, tool calibration physics, or vendor-specific chart interpretation, regulatory casing/cement requirements for any specific jurisdiction, well integrity diagnostics beyond cement (casing corrosion logs, pressure testing, SCP analysis)

**Scope:** Practitioner handbook guidance, not a research paper or vendor manual; generic and non-basin-specific, written around conventional acoustic CBL practice, so thresholds such as bond index cutoffs and required footage are rules of thumb that should be checked against current tool specs and local regulatory standards rather than cited as measured results.
