# petrowiki-cement-bond-logs

**Covers:** A PetroWiki-derived reference on cement bond logging and cement integrity evaluation, covering conventional acoustic CBL tools, variable density logs, and radial/ultrasonic cement-evaluation imagers.

**Authoritative for:** how a conventional CBL tool works (transmitter-receiver spacing, frequency, amplitude vs. full waveform), interpreting CBL amplitude curves, travel time, and variable density log (VDL) displays, distinguishing free pipe, partial bond, and good bond; picking top of cement, shear bond vs. hydraulic bond concepts and why cement placement matters, limitations of conventional CBL for channel detection and hydraulic seal confirmation, radial cement evaluation: ultrasonic acoustic impedance, casing resonance damping, pad and azimuthal array tools, combined casing inspection plus cement evaluation with ultrasonic imagers (e.g., CAST-V modes, log track layout), effects of tool eccentering on CBL response

**Not good for:** cement slurry design, additives, rheology, or cement chemistry, cementing job execution, displacement mechanics, or remedial squeeze design, open-hole formation evaluation logs (porosity, resistivity, saturation), quantitative well-integrity or barrier-verification regulatory compliance criteria, casing design, wellbore stress, or tubular strength calculations, modern deep-azimuthal or fiber-optic cement monitoring beyond the tools described, field-specific or basin-specific case histories and stratigraphy

**Scope:** Generic industry tool-physics and interpretation encyclopedia content, not tied to any basin or formation; vendor tool descriptions reflect late-1990s to 2000s wireline technology (CBL/VDL, SBT, CAST-V-class ultrasonic imagers) and may omit later-generation tools. Guidance is qualitative interpretation practice rather than standards-grade acceptance criteria; always confirm tool specifications and pass/fail thresholds against current service-company documentation and local regulations.
