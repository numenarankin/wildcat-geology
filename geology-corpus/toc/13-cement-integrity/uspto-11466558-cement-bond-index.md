# uspto-11466558-cement-bond-index

**Covers:** US Patent 11,466,558 B2, a granted patent describing inversion-based array processing of sonic waveforms to compute an unbiased cement bond index (BI/CBI) from LWD (logging-while-drilling) acoustic tools, using a casing–tool wave interference model to separate tool-wave contamination from the casing wave.

**Authoritative for:** definition and calculation of cement bond index (BI) from casing wave amplitude/attenuation, calibrated to free pipe and 100% bond, why LWD sonic cement bond logs are biased by tool-wave arrivals in the first arrival window, and why acoustic isolators are inadequate in drill collars, the casing–tool wave interference forward model: complex-amplitude/phase representation across a receiver array, tool wave template acquisition, free-casing calibration, inversion workflow minimizing misfit between measured and modeled multi-receiver amplitudes, non-uniqueness of the CBL objective function, and tool-wave amplitude constraints to regularize it, sensitivity of derived casing amplitude/bond index to tool wave amplitude error (e.g. effect of a 10% error), claimed system hardware and real-time downhole computation of bonding index (transmitters, receivers, downhole memory, information handling system), comparison of achievable bond index range for conventional single-receiver (R1) amplitude processing versus array inversion on field data

**Not good for:** wireline CBL/VDL tool specifications, ultrasonic imaging (USIT/isolation scanner) or segmented bond tools, cement slurry design, chemistry, set behavior, or remedial squeeze practice, microannulus diagnosis, channel geometry mapping, or azimuthal cement coverage interpretation, regulatory zonal-isolation compliance criteria or well-integrity policy thresholds, formation evaluation, sonic slowness/velocity logging for rock properties, or open-hole acoustics, case-study-grade field validation — field data appears only illustratively, with no basin, well, or regional detail, statistical benchmarking against other vendors' cement evaluation algorithms

**Scope:** Modern patent literature (granted 2022, US filing) written as intellectual-property disclosure, not a peer-reviewed study: claim language and idealized models dominate, quantitative results are illustrative rather than validated, and applicability is tied to array-receiver LWD sonic tools operating at typical CBL transmitter frequencies in cased hole. Amplitude-based bond index only; no depth-, geographic-, or lithology-specific calibration is provided, and cited prior art is limited to a small set of SPE/SPWLA LWD cement evaluation papers.
