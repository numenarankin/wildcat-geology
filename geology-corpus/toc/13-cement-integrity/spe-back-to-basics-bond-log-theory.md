# spe-back-to-basics-bond-log-theory

**Covers:** An SPE 'Back to Basics' tutorial presentation on cement bond log (CBL) theory and interpretation, covering amplitude, transit time, VDL, and radial bond measurements.

**Authoritative for:** what a cement bond log physically measures (acoustic amplitude, travel time, waveform), reading a Variable Density Log and distinguishing casing arrivals from formation arrivals, recognising free pipe versus good cement bond signatures on a CBL, why azimuthal/radial bond tools are needed to detect channeling that a conventional CBL averages out, basic quality-control checks and pitfalls in CBL interpretation

**Not good for:** quantitative cement bond index cut-offs tied to a specific operator's or regulator's standards, cement slurry design, additives, or placement engineering, tool-specific vendor calibration procedures, response charts, or serial-number specs, ultrasonic imaging tools (USIT/CBIL-class) beyond conceptual comparison, annular pressure integrity or sustained casing pressure diagnosis, formation evaluation, petrophysics, or reservoir interpretation, stratigraphy, basin, or regional geology of any kind

**Scope:** Generic, non-regional tutorial material on wellbore cement integrity logging; conceptual and instructional rather than a standards document or tool manual. Applicable to conventional cased-hole acoustic logging in general — use current vendor documentation and regulatory guidance for any decision-grade bond index thresholds or abandonment sign-off.
