# slb-defining-well-cementing-fundamentals

**Covers:** An SLB (Schlumberger) educational/reference article explaining well cementing fundamentals — primary and remedial cementing, hardware, additives, slurry design, evaluation, and advanced (flexible, self-healing) systems for cement-sheath and zonal-isolation integrity.

**Authoritative for:** definitions and workflow of a two-plug primary cementing job, function of cementing hardware: guide shoes, float shoes/collars, centralizers, bottom and top wiper plugs, purpose of common cement additives (accelerators, retarders, extenders, fluid-loss agents, fibers, flexible particles), concepts of remedial cementing (squeeze jobs, plugs) and cement-sheath failure mechanisms, introductory framing of cement evaluation and long-term zonal isolation, how self-healing and flexible cement systems are intended to work

**Not good for:** quantitative slurry design, mix-water calculations, or job-specific volume and rate engineering, log-based cement bond evaluation interpretation (CBL/VDL, ultrasonic imaging) at working detail, regulatory or jurisdictional cementing requirements (top-of-cement, barrier standards, abandonment rules), field case histories, basin-specific practice, or well-specific failure diagnosis, API/ISO cement class specifications, testing procedures, or lab QA protocols, academic citation of cement chemistry or mechanical-property research

**Scope:** Vendor-authored (SLB) glossary/explainer content: modern oilfield practice, global and generic rather than tied to any basin, era, or operator. Terminology and product framing lean toward SLB technology; treat advanced-system claims as marketing-adjacent and verify against primary standards (API/ISO) or peer-reviewed sources before engineering or regulatory use.
