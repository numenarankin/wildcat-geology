# uspto-10392920-cement-attenuation-bond-index

**Covers:** US Patent 10,392,920 B2, a granted patent describing quantitative cement evaluation (attenuation, casing amplitude, bond index) from logging-while-drilling sonic waveform data.

**Authoritative for:** definition and computation of bond index from acoustic attenuation rates, converting long transmitter-receiver spacing (e.g. 7 ft) LWD casing amplitude to equivalent 3 ft CBL amplitude, normalizing casing amplitude when no free-pipe interval is available, recognizing and mitigating tool extensional / collar mode contamination in LWD cement logs, time-lapse LWD cement bond monitoring while tripping in/out of hole, top-of-cement identification from LWD sonic data, the specific claims and priority chain of US 10,392,920

**Not good for:** wireline CBL/VDL tool hardware specifications or vendor service descriptions, ultrasonic (pulse-echo, flexural) cement imaging interpretation, cement slurry chemistry, mix design, or placement engineering, regional geology, formation evaluation, or petrophysical porosity/saturation workflows, field case studies with statistically validated results — this is patent disclosure, not peer-reviewed benchmarking, regulatory compliance criteria for zonal isolation

**Scope:** Modern (2010s, granted 2019) US patent literature; borehole geophysics / cased-hole acoustics domain only, not basin- or era-specific. Content is a legal disclosure written to support claims: workflows are described in enabling but idealized terms, cited prior art (e.g. Grosmangin's original CBL paper) is listed rather than reviewed, and quantitative thresholds should be corroborated against service-company or SPE sources before operational use.
