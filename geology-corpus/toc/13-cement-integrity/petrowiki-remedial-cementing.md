**Covers:** A PetroWiki article on remedial cementing operations, covering squeeze cementing techniques and cement plug placement for wellbore repair and zonal isolation.

**Authoritative for:** squeeze cementing methods (running vs. hesitation, high- vs. low-pressure, Bradenhead), when remedial cementing is indicated versus other repair options, cement plug applications: zonal isolation, water shutoff, formation testing, wellbore stability, use of bridge plugs and squeeze packers/retainers to support plugs, alternative plugging agents to neat cement

**Not good for:** primary cementing design, slurry rheology, or additive chemistry, cement bond log / ultrasonic log interpretation and cement evaluation workflows, abandonment regulations or jurisdiction-specific P&A requirements, quantitative job design: volumes, pressures, pump schedules for a specific well, geology, stratigraphy, formation properties, or reservoir characterization, cost estimates, equipment specifications, or vendor products

**Scope:** General-practice engineering reference, not region- or basin-specific and not tied to a single operator's standards; wiki-style summary text with no field data, case histories, or regulatory citations. Treat as orientation and terminology, not as a design specification or compliance authority.
