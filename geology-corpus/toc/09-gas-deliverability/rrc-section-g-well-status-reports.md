# rrc-section-g-well-status-reports

**Covers:** A Texas Railroad Commission forms manual excerpt (Section G and adjacent) reproducing blank forms, filing instructions, and Q&A for well status reporting, multiple completions, cementing, inactive-well testing, plugging, and abandoned-well conversion.

**Authoritative for:** Texas RRC Form W-10 oil well status report: when to file, testing cycles, delinquency lookup, test procedure and reporting units, Texas RRC Form G-10 gas well status report and deliverability test: pre-flow stabilization, 24-hour test duration, filing deadlines, SIWH and other report terms, Casinghead gas base pressure and temperature reporting conventions on W-10, Multiple completion applications (Forms W-4, W-4A, W-5, W-6): required attachments, filing address, fees, offset operator notice, alphabetic completion codes and historical code mappings, Communication / packer leakage test procedure for dual and triple completions, including shut-in stabilization times and pressure gauge capacity/accuracy requirements, Form W-15 cementing report: operator vs. cementer responsibilities, plug data, top of cement, WOC hours, which forms it attaches to, copy counts, dry-hole applicability, Form P-6 lease subdivision/consolidation filing, plat copies, and interaction with Form P-4, Form H-15 inactive well fluid level testing and mechanical integrity testing under Statewide Rule 14(b)(2)/(3), wellbore age determination, MIT validity period, Form W-3A notice of intention to plug (advance notice days, copies, approval validity) and Form W-3 plugging record fields, Form P-13 landowner application to condition an abandoned well for fresh water production and its accompanying filings

**Not good for:** Regulatory requirements outside Texas or for federal/OCS lands, Current forms, fees, e-filing portals, or deadlines — form versions and RRC procedures change, so verify against the live RRC site, Reservoir engineering interpretation: deliverability curve analysis, IPR/AOF modeling, decline analysis, or reserves estimation, Petrophysical calculation, log analysis, or core/fluid property data, Geological description: stratigraphy, structure, depositional setting, or basin history, Allowable/proration rule derivations, field rules, or spacing and density regulations beyond what these forms reference, Drilling, completion, or cementing engineering design guidance (it records what was done, not how to design it), Environmental permitting or TCEQ groundwater requirements beyond the referenced letter for P-13

**Scope:** Texas Railroad Commission oil and gas administrative practice only; procedural/forms-manual content with no geologic or quantitative-analysis material. Undated reproduction of blank forms and instruction sheets, so treat all deadlines, form revisions, fee amounts, district office addresses, and rule citations as historical and subject to supersession by current RRC rules.
