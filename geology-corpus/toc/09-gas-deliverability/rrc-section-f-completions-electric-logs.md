# rrc-section-f-completions-electric-logs

**Covers:** Texas Railroad Commission Section F regulatory filing package covering oil and gas well completion reports (W-2, G-1), gas well classification and status (G-5, G-10), proration/inclination/log/cementing forms (P-15, W-12, L-1, W-15), plus Rule 86 horizontal drainhole displacement calculation procedures.

**Authoritative for:** Which RRC form and Statewide Rule applies to a Texas oil or gas well completion filing (W-2, W-12, W-15, L-1, G-1, G-5, G-10, P-15, P-16), Filing deadlines, copies required, and signature/certification requirements for each RRC completion-related form, Form field-by-field content: potential test data, casing/liner/tubing records, frac items, formation record (item 45), cementing slurry details, Texas initial potential test and back-pressure test procedures (four-point vs one-point), swabbing rules, allowable for pre-test oil, RRC gas well vs oil well classification criteria: GOR cutoff under Statewide Rule 79, oil gravity, ASTM distillation limits, heptanes-plus option, PVT test requirements, G-10 deliverability test exemption thresholds and BHP / shut-in wellhead pressure reporting, Horizontal drainhole displacement (HDD) and effective HDD calculation under Statewide Rule 86, including penetration point, take points, multiple/stacked laterals, and worked vector examples, Inclination survey requirements: shot-point spacing, accumulative displacement limits, W-12 filing triggers, What qualifies as a basic electric log for §3.16 filing, log header required items, and log confidentiality periods, Cementing report practice: cement not circulated to surface, temperature survey / cement bond log requirements

**Not good for:** Regulatory requirements outside Texas — other states, federal/BLM, or offshore jurisdictions, Log interpretation, petrophysical calculation, or quantitative formation evaluation (it governs log filing, not log analysis), Reservoir engineering estimates, reserves, or decline analysis beyond the reported test values, Stratigraphic nomenclature, regional geology, or basin history; formation tops appear only as a reporting field, Directional drilling engineering design or survey tool accuracy/QC theory beyond the prescribed HDD arithmetic, Permitting before drilling (W-1), plugging procedure detail, or environmental/waste rules, Current fee schedules, e-filing mechanics, or rule amendments made after the document's edition

**Scope:** Texas Railroad Commission jurisdiction only, onshore Texas oil and gas wells; a procedural/administrative forms manual reflecting the Statewide Rules as of its issue date, so verify current rule text, thresholds, and deadlines against the live RRC rules before relying on numeric cutoffs. Contains one example wireline log header (Diamondback Spruce Trails 40-6, Permian) purely as an illustration, not as a regional dataset.
