# tamu-png613-lec07-deliverability-testing-gas-wells

**Covers:** University lecture notes (Texas A&M PNG 613, Lecture 7) on gas well deliverability testing, covering AOF potential, backpressure curves, and the Rawlins-Schellhardt and Houpeurt/Forchheimer analysis equations, with worked examples and Cullender's isochronal test data.

**Authoritative for:** definition and calculation of absolute open-flow (AOF) potential, flow-after-flow (conventional 4-point) deliverability test procedure and interpretation, Rawlins and Schellhardt empirical backpressure equation and the meaning of C and n, Houpeurt/Forchheimer laminar-inertial-turbulent (LIT) pseudopressure form aq + bq^2, isochronal and modified isochronal test concepts, including stabilization time behavior, worked tabulations of multipoint test data and coefficient-of-performance variation with flow duration

**Not good for:** oil well or multiphase deliverability and inflow performance, pressure transient buildup/drawdown interpretation beyond what deliverability testing requires, gas reserves estimation, material balance, or reservoir simulation, condensate banking, liquid loading, or wellbore hydraulics and tubing performance, field-specific or basin-specific production data, modern digital well test acquisition hardware, regulatory testing rules, or unconventional/shale gas multi-fractured horizontal well deliverability

**Scope:** Classroom teaching material rooted in classical mid-20th-century gas well test theory (Rawlins & Schellhardt 1935, Cullender 1955, Houpeurt); assumes dry gas, single-phase, vertical wells in conventional radial-flow reservoirs, and uses oilfield units. Examples are illustrative teaching datasets, not a regional or field study; verify equations and constants against a current well testing reference before engineering use.
