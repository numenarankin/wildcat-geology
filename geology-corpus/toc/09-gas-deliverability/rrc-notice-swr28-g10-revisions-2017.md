**Covers:** A Texas Railroad Commission operator notice describing the January 1, 2017 amendments to Statewide Rule 28 and the accompanying revisions to Form G-10 (gas well back-pressure/deliverability test reporting).

**Authoritative for:** Texas gas well deliverability (back-pressure) test requirements and testing frequency under SWR 28 as amended effective 1 Jan 2017, When retesting is triggered by recompletion into a different field, Form G-10 filing content, including whether shut-in wellhead pressure may be calculated rather than measured, Summary of what specifically changed in SWR 28 in the 2017 revision
