# petrowiki-gas-well-performance

**Covers:** A PetroWiki reference chapter on gas well performance and deliverability, covering Darcy-law flow equations for gas, real-gas pseudopressure, transient and pseudosteady-state flow, and backpressure/isochronal deliverability test analysis.

**Authoritative for:** steady-state radial gas flow equations and the real-gas potential (pseudopressure) formulation, when μg·z or μg·Bg can be treated as constant in gas flow equations, radial diffusivity equation, infinite-acting log approximation, and the four flow periods of a pressure transient test, pseudosteady-state gas flow and Dietz shape factors for various drainage shapes, transient drainage radius, radius of investigation, and identifying the transition to pseudosteady state, four-point/stabilized backpressure test analysis: determining C and n, interpreting slope 1/n, computing AOF, isochronal and modified isochronal test design and plotting (Δp² vs qg, Δψ/qsc vs qsc), skin and non-Darcy (turbulent) flow coefficient D in deliverability equations, standard nomenclature and classic references for gas deliverability equations

**Not good for:** oil or multiphase well inflow performance (IPR) and Vogel-type analysis, liquid loading, wellbore hydraulics, tubing/nodal analysis, or surface facility constraints, detailed well test interpretation of complex geometries: fractured, horizontal, dual-porosity, or layered reservoir type curves, gas PVT correlations, z-factor determination, or fluid property measurement, condensate banking, retrograde condensate, or compositional effects near the wellbore, reservoir-specific field data, basin-specific case histories, or regional stratigraphy, modern numerical simulation, rate-transient analysis, or unconventional shale gas decline methods, volumetric or material-balance reserve estimation

**Scope:** Classical analytical single-phase dry-gas well performance theory (Darcy/Al-Hussainy-Ramey lineage) as compiled in PetroWiki from SPE textbook material; assumes homogeneous, vertical-well, single-layer radial geometry and oilfield units. Not basin- or country-specific, and not intended to reflect post-2010 rate-transient or unconventional-reservoir practice.
