# rrc-section-k-gas-proration-allowables

**Covers:** Texas Railroad Commission Section K manual material on gas well proration: allowable supplements, allowable/disposition code keys, proration schedules and ledgers, balancing under Statewide Rule 31, deliverability/capability determination (Forms G-10, GC-1), and AOF status applications (Forms AOF-1/2/3).

**Authoritative for:** meaning of RRC gas allowable codes (R, N, OA, K2, #) and disposition codes on proration schedules and gas production ledgers, how gas allowables are assigned by field classification: prorated, non-prorated one-well, 49B associated, statewide exempt (250 MCFD), administrative vs hearing specials, gas balancing dates, balancing periods, overproduction makeup deadlines, cancelled underage, and overproduction letter types under Rule 31(h)(i), Form G-10 deliverability filing and delinquency consequences; Form GC-1 substitute well capability determination, filing deadlines, certification and duration, acreage factor and market demand forecast steps in prorated field allowable allocation to individual wells, AOF (absolute open flow) field status procedures under Rule 31(j): Forms AOF-1, AOF-2, AOF-3, active-well definition, annual recertification, objections, return to prorated status, where and when RRC gas proration schedules, operator/gatherer/purchaser strip outs are published

**Not good for:** oil proration, oil allowables, or casinghead gas rules (different RRC section), reservoir engineering theory of deliverability, AOF/back-pressure test analysis, or nodal analysis math, geology, stratigraphy, field designation criteria, or reserves estimation, regulatory practice outside Texas or before other agencies (BLM, state commissions), current statutory/rule text, fee amounts, filing portals, or form versions — treat as a snapshot needing verification against current RRC rules and forms, well completion, permitting (W-1/W-2), or production reporting mechanics beyond allowable/ledger interaction

**Scope:** Administrative/regulatory procedure only, limited to Texas RRC gas proration under Statewide Rule 31 and H.B. 1866-era practice; reflects a specific vintage of RRC manual, forms, and paper/tape recordkeeping (months of allowables kept on tape), so filing addresses, deadlines, and code lists may be outdated.
