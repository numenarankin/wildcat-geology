# petrowiki-peh-inflow-outflow-performance

**Covers:** A PetroWiki Petroleum Engineering Handbook chapter on inflow and outflow performance — gas well deliverability testing, oil well IPR correlations, wellbore/tubing flow, chokes, and nodal analysis.

**Authoritative for:** gas well deliverability equations: Rawlins-Schellhardt backpressure (C, n) and Houpeurt (a, b) forms, calculating AOF (absolute open flow) from multirate test data, designing and analyzing flow-after-flow, isochronal, modified isochronal, and multiple modified isochronal deliverability tests, forecasting future gas well deliverability as reservoir pressure declines; non-Darcy coefficient D and turbulence factor beta estimation, oil well IPR construction below the bubblepoint: Vogel, Fetkovich, Klins & Majcher, Sukarno, Wiggins, composite IPR, Jones-Blount-Glaze, constant terminal-rate radial diffusivity solution for steady-state single-phase inflow, tubing performance curves and pressure-drop components (elevation, acceleration, friction), single-phase gas flow in tubing (average temperature and z-factor method), multiphase flow regimes in tubing and selection among empirical correlations; pressure-traverse curves, choke sizing, critical flow criteria, and critical pressure ratio, systems (nodal) analysis: node placement, inflow/outflow curve intersection and its absence, liquid-dominated geothermal well inflow performance and thermal power output

**Not good for:** pressure transient / well test interpretation for permeability and skin (buildup, drawdown analysis), reservoir simulation, material balance, or reserves estimation, surface facility, flowline network, and separator design beyond the choke, artificial lift design details (pump sizing, gas lift valve spacing) — lift is only motivated, not designed, unconventional/multi-fractured horizontal well or transient linear-flow production analysis, rock physics, petrophysics, log analysis, stratigraphy, or basin geology, modern mechanistic multiphase flow models and commercial software workflows, field- or basin-specific case histories and local calibration factors

**Scope:** Handbook/tutorial chapter grounded in classical mid- to late-20th-century petroleum engineering practice (Rawlins & Schellhardt 1935 through Fetkovich, Vogel, Jones-Blount-Glaze and 1980s-90s IPR work); vertical/near-vertical conventional oil and gas wells, radial flow, oilfield units; empirical correlations with stated validity ranges rather than mechanistic models; equations are generic and not tied to any particular basin, but assume stabilized or idealized test conditions that low-permeability and unconventional wells may violate.
