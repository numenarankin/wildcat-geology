# rrc-g10-instructions

**Covers:** Texas Railroad Commission Form G-10 (Gas Well Status Report) filing instructions, covering when the report is due and how gas well deliverability tests must be run and reported.

**Authoritative for:** Form G-10 filing triggers, deadlines, and who must file, required duration of a gas well deliverability test, pre-flow/stabilization period before taking a G-10 test, which measured rate to report as the daily deliverability volume

**Not good for:** reservoir engineering theory or derivation of deliverability/IPR curves, well test interpretation, pressure-transient or buildup analysis, allowable/proration calculations, gas allocation formulas, or other RRC forms (G-1, G-5, P-4, etc.), regulations of other states or federal/offshore leases, completion, plugging, or production reporting procedures

**Scope:** Texas-only regulatory procedure under Railroad Commission of Texas statewide rules; it is administrative form guidance, not technical or geologic analysis, and reflects the rule version current to the instruction sheet — verify against the current RRC-published form before relying on deadlines or test durations.
