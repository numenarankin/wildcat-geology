# directional-drilling-advanced-trajectory-modelling-thesis

**Covers:** A university thesis on advanced directional drilling trajectory modelling: survey calculation methods, well path planning functions (build & turn, tangent, curve-to-target), MWD survey principles, error models and anti-collision separation factors, with MATLAB implementations validated against Compass software.

**Authoritative for:** formulas and derivations for survey calculation methods: minimum curvature (dogleg angle, ratio factor F), radius of curvature, tangential, balanced tangential, constant turn rate, constant curvature, computing dogleg severity, build/turn rates, radius of curvature from deg/30 m, and curve length of a build section, well path planning functions: build-and-turn to a target TVD, tangent/hold departures, dogleg toolface angle relations, curve-to-tangent-to-target geometry, azimuth handling details: wrapping past 0/360, quadrant/zone-change checks, target azimuth alignment during 3D curves, why the Exact Departures Constant Turn Rate equation degenerates when build rate equals turn rate, MWD survey mathematics: converting Gx/Gy/Gz to inclination and magnetometer+accelerometer readings to azimuth, anti-collision separation factor calculation, typical guideline values, and when it is applied in planning vs execution, comparing method accuracy and known limitations/anomalies of commercial planning software (Compass), nomenclature and symbol conventions for directional trajectory calculations, MATLAB code for a build-and-turn planning function

**Not good for:** field-specific or basin-specific well data, formation geology, or stratigraphy, drilling hydraulics, mud engineering, casing/cement design, BHA mechanics, torque and drag, or bit selection, detailed ISCWSA/OWSG error model coefficients and rigorous positional uncertainty ellipsoid computation (surveying errors are treated only at an introductory level), gyro, wireline, or rotary steerable tool hardware specifications and performance, petrophysics, log interpretation, or reserves estimation, regulatory or operational anti-collision procedures for a specific operator or jurisdiction, geosteering and real-time formation-based trajectory adjustment

**Scope:** Academic MSc-level thesis, geometry- and mathematics-driven rather than data-driven; conclusions are validated against one commercial package (Landmark Compass) rather than field measurements. Uses metric units and deg/30 m build-rate convention (convert for deg/100 ft practice). Method-general and not tied to any basin, operator, or well; treat software-anomaly observations as version-specific to the Compass release used at the time of writing.

_12 segments._
