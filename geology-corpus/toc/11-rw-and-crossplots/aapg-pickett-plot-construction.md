# aapg-pickett-plot-construction

**Covers:** A how-to guide on constructing and reading Pickett plots (log-log porosity vs. deep resistivity crossplots) for estimating Rw, cementation exponent m, and water saturation from wireline logs.

**Authoritative for:** step-by-step Pickett plot construction from porosity and deep resistivity logs, axis conventions (Rt on x, porosity on y, both log scales) and plotting the Rw anchor point, estimating cementation exponent m from the slope of the water-bearing trend when core data are unavailable, graphical Rw determination and Archie-based Sw interpretation from crossplot position

**Not good for:** core-measured electrical properties (formation factor, m and n from special core analysis), shaly-sand saturation models (Waxman-Smits, dual-water, Indonesia) where clean-sand Archie assumptions fail, carbonate or fractured-reservoir petrophysics with strongly variable m, log acquisition, environmental corrections, or tool physics, reservoir volumetrics, mapping, depositional or stratigraphic interpretation, basin- or field-specific parameter values

**Scope:** Method-level petrophysical technique, not tied to a basin, formation, or age; assumes clean, water-bearing intervals of relatively constant Rw and lithology and a valid Archie relationship. Results are graphical estimates whose reliability depends on data scatter and on the presence of a genuine 100% water-bearing trend.
