# aapg-water-resistivity-determination

**Covers:** An AAPG-style methods chapter on determining formation water resistivity (Rw) from well logs, covering SP-based, Archie/resistivity-ratio, and Rxo-based approaches plus crossplot techniques.

**Authoritative for:** Calculating Rw from SP log deflection (SSP, Rwe, Rw conversions), The K constant in the SSP equation and its temperature dependence, Interpreting anomalous or reversed SP deflections, Resistivity-ratio and Archie-based Rw estimation in clean water-bearing zones, Rxo/flushed-zone method for Rw and its failure modes, Cross-checking and reconciling Rw values from multiple methods

**Not good for:** Direct chemical analysis of produced water samples or water chemistry interpretation, Saturation calculation workflows beyond the Rw input step, Shaly-sand saturation models (Waxman-Smits, dual-water) and clay-conductivity corrections, NMR, dielectric, or pulsed-neutron log interpretation, Basin-specific or field-specific water salinity data, Modern digital/AI log-processing workflows

**Scope:** Conventional open-hole wireline log interpretation, classic chart-book era methodology (temperature-corrected Rw charts, SP departure curves); assumes clean, water-bearing, moderately permeable formations and freshwater/saltwater mud systems. Generic rather than tied to any one basin; results degrade in shaly, low-porosity, or fresh-formation-water settings and where mud filtrate resistivity has changed since drilling.
