# usgs-produced-waters-geochemical-database-v3

**Covers:** Landing-page/citation-level record for the USGS National Produced Waters Geochemical Database version 3.0, a public compilation of produced water (formation brine) chemistry from U.S. oil and gas wells, framed here for Rw estimation and crossplot workflows.

**Authoritative for:** locating and citing the USGS Produced Waters Geochemical Database v3.0 (DOI, data release URL, version history vs. 2.3), confirming that a public U.S. formation-water chemistry compilation exists and what it broadly contains (TDS, major ions, sample locations, formation attribution), sourcing measured brine salinity as an input to Rw / Archie water-saturation calculations

**Not good for:** the actual data values — no tables, analyses, or per-formation salinity numbers are reproduced here, petrophysical method detail: Rw-from-salinity conversion equations, temperature corrections, Archie parameter selection, or crossplot construction steps, non-U.S. basins, or any basin where produced water sampling is sparse, water chemistry outside oil and gas produced water (potable groundwater, injection water quality, geothermal fluids), data quality screening — the database mixes decades of analyses of uneven reliability and this card does not document QC flags

**Scope:** United States only, oil and gas produced water samples aggregated from many legacy and modern sources spanning most of the 20th century into the 2010s; this is a metadata/pointer entry, so treat it as a gateway to the USGS data release rather than a source of values or methods.
