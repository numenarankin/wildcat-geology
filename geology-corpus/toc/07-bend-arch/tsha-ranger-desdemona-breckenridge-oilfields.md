# tsha-ranger-desdemona-breckenridge-oilfields

**Covers:** A Texas State Historical Association-style historical account of the Ranger, Desdemona, and Breckenridge oil discoveries on the Bend Arch of North Texas (1917-1920s boom era).

**Authoritative for:** discovery dates and circumstances of the Ranger, Desdemona, and Breckenridge oilfields, the McClesky No. 1 discovery well near Ranger and its completion depth, peak production figures and boom-town history for Breckenridge and Desdemona, who financed and drilled the early deep tests in the Ranger area

**Not good for:** reservoir engineering, petrophysical, or log-based calculations, modern production or reserves figures and current field status, detailed stratigraphy, structural interpretation, or source-rock geochemistry of the Bend Arch, analogues for fields outside North Central Texas

**Scope:** North Central Texas, Bend Arch province (Eastland, Stephens, and Comanche county area); early 20th-century boom history written as regional-encyclopedia narrative, not technical geoscience, so numbers are historical and may be approximate.
