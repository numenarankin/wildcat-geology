# eargle-1960-usgs-pp-315d

**Covers:** USGS Professional Paper 315-D (Eargle, 1960) — a stratigraphic nomenclature and correlation report on Pennsylvanian to Lower Permian outcrops along the Colorado River valley of central Texas (Brown, Coleman, Mills, San Saba Counties), on the Bend arch flank.

**Authoritative for:** formal nomenclature, type localities, and naming history of Strawn, Canyon, Cisco, and Wichita group units in the Colorado River valley of central Texas, member-level subdivision of the Graford, Brad, Caddo Creek, Graham, Thrifty, and Pueblo formations, outcrop thicknesses and lithologic descriptions of named limestone, shale, sandstone, and conglomerate members (Capps, Ricker Station, Brownwood, Adams Branch, Cedarton, Winchell, Placid, Ranger, Colony Creek, Home Creek, Bluff Creek, Gunsight, Wayland, Ivan, Breckenridge, Chaffin, Waldrip, Saddle Creek), correlation of central Texas Strawn/Canyon/Cisco units with Brazos River valley equivalents (Mineral Wells, Millsap, Palo Pinto, Graham type sections), placement of the Pennsylvanian–Permian system boundary at the base of the Waldrip shale member, with supporting fusulinid evidence, resolving conflicting older nomenclature (e.g., Hog Creek vs. Colony Creek, Trickham vs. Wayland, Bellerophon vs. Ivan, Avis sandstone usage), channel sandstones and unconformities such as the Kisinger channel and the Overall oil sand in Coleman County

**Not good for:** basin-scale structural or tectonic analysis of the Bend arch or Fort Worth Basin, reservoir engineering, petrophysics, log analysis, or production data, modern sequence-stratigraphic or chronostratigraphic frameworks and current ICS stage assignments, biostratigraphy beyond incidental fusulinid and megafossil citations used for correlation, subsurface mapping outside Brown and Coleman Counties, or deep-basin facies of the Midland/Permian Basin, depositional-systems interpretation, provenance, geochemistry, or diagenesis, any area outside central and north-central Texas

**Scope:** 1960 USGS outcrop stratigraphy, limited to the Colorado River valley of central Texas (chiefly Brown and Coleman, plus Mills and San Saba Counties) on the Bend arch, covering Desmoinesian through early Permian (Wolfcampian) strata. Nomenclature is mid-20th-century USGS usage — group and formation names, boundary picks, and 'Des Moines/Missouri' series terminology predate modern revisions and should be cross-checked against current Texas stratigraphic lexicons before citation.
