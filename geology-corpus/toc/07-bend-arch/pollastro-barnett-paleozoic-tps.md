# pollastro-barnett-paleozoic-tps

**Covers:** USGS chapter defining the Barnett–Paleozoic Total Petroleum System of the Bend Arch–Fort Worth Basin (Province 045), covering source-rock geochemistry, thermal maturity, reservoirs, seals, and the continuous and conventional assessment units.

**Authoritative for:** Barnett Shale source-rock character: TOC ranges, kerogen type, Rock-Eval/maturity windows (oil, wet gas, dry gas Ro thresholds), Total petroleum system framework and assessment-unit definitions for USGS Province 045, Bend Arch–Fort Worth Basin structural framework, stratigraphy, and Paleozoic production history, Barnett Shale reservoir properties: mineralogy of productive facies, porosity/permeability, frac barriers (Forestburg, Viola-Simpson, Ellenburger), Why Newark East / Greater Newark East is a gas 'sweet spot' and why fault systems (Mineral Wells, Newark East) degrade production, Hydrocarbon migration from Barnett into overlying/underlying Paleozoic reservoirs across the Bend Arch, Distinction between play-based and TPS assessment methods; definition of continuous-type accumulations

**Not good for:** Undiscovered resource numbers themselves (the assessment results chapter, not this geologic framework chapter, and figures are superseded by later USGS reassessments), Modern horizontal-well completion design, proppant/fluid engineering, or refracturing practice, Post-2007 production statistics, EUR revisions, or the mature Barnett decline history, Other shale plays (Marcellus, Eagle Ford, Haynesville) or other basins — no analog treatment, Petrophysical log-interpretation workflows or well-by-well quantitative log models, Induced seismicity, water disposal, or environmental issues

**Scope:** USGS assessment-era publication (mid-2000s, Barnett Shale boom onset) limited to the Bend Arch–Fort Worth Basin, north-central Texas; Mississippian Barnett source rock and Paleozoic (Ordovician–Pennsylvanian) reservoirs. Data reflect vertical-to-early-horizontal drilling; EURs, sweet-spot extent, and resource estimates predate full-field horizontal development and have been revised by later work.
