# feray-brooks-pennsylvanian-canyon-stratigraphy-north-central-texas

**Covers:** A stratigraphic treatment of the Pennsylvanian Strawn, Canyon and Cisco Groups in the outcrop belt of North Central Texas (Bend Arch region), emphasizing formation/member nomenclature and lateral facies changes.

**Authoritative for:** lithostratigraphic nomenclature of the Strawn, Canyon and Cisco Groups in North Central Texas, type localities and outcrop occurrences of units such as the Lake Pinto Sandstone, Brazos River Sandstone, Village Bend and Capps Limestones, lateral facies changes and pinch-outs of the Winchell and Home Creek Limestones (e.g. northeastward into Jack County), relationship of the Upper and Lower Cundiff Limestones to the Home Creek and Ranger limestones, nature of the Canyon–Cisco (Home Creek–Graham) contact, the Kissinger Channel and its exposure, rationale for group-level rather than series-level subdivision, and why Canyon Group formations/members are hard to define, fusulinid marker for the base of the Missourian relative to the Palo Pinto Limestone

**Not good for:** subsurface correlation, well-log petrophysics or reservoir property estimates, source-rock geochemistry, maturity or production data, modern sequence-stratigraphic, chemostratigraphic or radiometric age frameworks, Pennsylvanian stratigraphy outside North Central Texas (Midland Basin, Anadarko Basin, Permian Basin platform interiors), detailed biostratigraphic zonation beyond a few fusulinid markers, structural or basin-modeling analysis of the Fort Worth Basin or Bend Arch

**Scope:** Pennsylvanian (Desmoinesian–Virgilian) outcrop belt of North Central Texas along the Bend Arch / western Fort Worth Basin margin — Mineral Wells, Brazos River, Palo Pinto and Jack County areas; a mid-20th-century-style outcrop nomenclature and facies study, so unit names and correlations may predate current formal usage and it carries no quantitative subsurface or geochemical data.

_6 segments._
