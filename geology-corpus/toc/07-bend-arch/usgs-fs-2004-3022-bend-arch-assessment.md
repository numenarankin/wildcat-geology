# usgs-fs-2004-3022-bend-arch-assessment

**Covers:** A short USGS Fact Sheet (FS 2004-3022) presenting the 2003 assessment of undiscovered, technically recoverable oil and gas resources in the Bend Arch-Fort Worth Basin Province, Texas, including the Barnett Shale continuous gas assessment units.

**Authoritative for:** USGS 2003 mean/probabilistic estimates of undiscovered gas, oil, and NGL in the Bend Arch-Fort Worth Basin Province, names and definitions of the assessment units and total petroleum systems used for the Barnett Shale and Bend arch conventional plays, province-scale framing of the Barnett Shale as a continuous (unconventional) gas accumulation as understood circa 2003

**Not good for:** current or post-2003 Barnett Shale resource and reserve numbers (superseded by later USGS assessments and actual production history), well-level or field-level engineering data, completion or fracture design, decline curves, EURs, detailed stratigraphy, core descriptions, geochemistry, or petrophysical parameters and cutoffs, economics, drilling costs, or reserve growth analysis, any basin outside the Bend Arch-Fort Worth Basin Province

**Scope:** North-central Texas only (Fort Worth Basin and Bend arch); assessment vintage 2003, published 2004, predating the bulk of horizontal Barnett development, so volumes are conservative relative to later assessments; fact-sheet length means methodology and geologic support are summarized rather than documented.
