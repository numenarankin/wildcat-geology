# usgs-ofr-2007-1313-young-county

**Covers:** USGS Open-File Report 2007-1313: a data-driven study of vitrinite reflectance (Ro) and rank of Pennsylvanian coals in the shallow subsurface of Young County, north-central Texas, plus an extensive appendix tabulating coal and dark-gray shale occurrences in several hundred old drill-cuttings well records.

**Authoritative for:** vitrinite reflectance (Ro) values measured on coal and coaly shale from drill cuttings in Young County, Texas wells, coal rank assignments for Pennsylvanian Strawn, Canyon, and Cisco Group coals in Young County, stratigraphic placement of sampled coalbeds using old electric logs and the definitions used for tops of the Strawn, Canyon, and Cisco Groups locally, log-response character of thin coalbeds on vintage 1 inch = 100 ft electric/resistivity logs, including why some coals show no resistivity deflection, well-by-well depths of coal, black shale, and dark-gray shale reported in drill cuttings for ~587 occurrences in Young County, legacy well metadata for Young County: operator, lease/well name, survey-block-abstract location, surface elevation, total depth, spud and completion dates, dry hole vs. producer status, methods for digitizing legacy log curves and constructing a stratigraphic cross section from them

**Not good for:** regional or basin-wide thermal maturity or burial history modeling beyond Young County, coal resource tonnage, quality, ash/sulfur, or mineability assessments, coalbed methane gas content, adsorption isotherms, or producibility estimates, source-rock geochemistry (TOC, Rock-Eval, kerogen typing) or oil/gas generation modeling, modern petrophysical analysis, log-based porosity/saturation calculations, or reservoir characterization, regional Pennsylvanian stratigraphic correlation or formal nomenclature revision outside north-central Texas, structural or tectonic interpretation of the Bend Arch–Fort Worth Basin beyond local setting notes, production volumes, reserves, or field performance data

**Scope:** Pennsylvanian (Strawn, Canyon, Cisco Groups) shallow subsurface of Young County, Texas, on the Bend Arch / eastern shelf margin; data come from archived drill cuttings and vintage electric logs of wells drilled mostly ca. 1929–1952, so depths and formation tops carry legacy-log uncertainty and Ro values were measured on only a subset of samples (some barren of indigenous vitrinite or containing recycled vitrinite). A 2007 open-file report: data compilation, not a peer-reviewed interpretive synthesis, and not extendable to other counties or basins.
