# jas-water-coning-iranian-gas-field

**Covers:** A study of water coning in a bottom-water-drive gas well, combining coning theory and critical-rate correlations with numerical sensitivity simulations based on the Sarkhoon gas field, Iran.

**Authoritative for:** water coning mechanism and controlling factors in gas wells with bottom-water aquifers, critical rate correlations (Meyer & Garder, Schols) and their application to gas-water systems, sensitivity of water cut and cumulative water production to completion standoff above the gas-water contact, gas rate, turbulence (non-Darcy flow), and skin factor, near-wellbore consequences of water invasion: bottom-hole pressure decline at constant gas rate, residual/trapped gas saturation in water-invaded blocks, practical options for controlling or reducing water production in gas wells

**Not good for:** oil-well coning design or oil critical-rate matching beyond the cited generic correlations, edge-water or lateral aquifer encroachment, gas cap coning, or dual-coning problems, horizontal or multilateral well coning behavior, water shutoff chemistry, gel/cement squeeze design details, or artificial-lift/deliquification engineering, reservoir-scale material balance, reserves estimation, or field development economics, transfer of quantitative sensitivity results to reservoirs with different permeability, anisotropy, thickness, or fluid properties

**Scope:** Modern simulation-based study anchored to one carbonate-type Iranian gas field (Sarkhoon, Persian Gulf region) with a bottom-water aquifer; numerical results are model- and field-specific, and rate cases span roughly 20-50 MMSCFD. Correlations quoted are classical vertical-well analytical forms with known simplifying assumptions.
