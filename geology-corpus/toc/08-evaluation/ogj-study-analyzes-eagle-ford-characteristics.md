# ogj-study-analyzes-eagle-ford-characteristics

**Covers:** An Oil & Gas Journal study section on Eagle Ford Shale evaluation, focused on quantifying total organic carbon from wireline logs using bulk-density and Passey delta log R methods.

**Authoritative for:** calculating TOC from bulk density logs in the Eagle Ford, applying and calibrating the delta log R (Passey) resistivity–sonic overlay method, selecting organic-lean baselines for delta log R overlays, understanding why delta log R underestimates TOC at high thermal maturity

**Not good for:** core-based geochemistry workflows (Rock-Eval pyrolysis, LECO TOC procedures), reservoir engineering, completion design, or production forecasting, stratigraphy, depositional setting, or regional correlation of the Eagle Ford, mineralogy, brittleness, or geomechanical property estimation, porosity, saturation, or permeability petrophysics, applying the calibrations directly to other shale plays without local rework

**Scope:** Trade-press (Oil & Gas Journal) study, Eagle Ford Shale of South Texas; log-based organic-richness estimation only. Baselines and density–TOC coefficients are locally calibrated and maturity-dependent, so numbers should be re-derived for other basins or even other Eagle Ford maturity windows. Not a peer-reviewed source; check the publication date before using it as current practice.
