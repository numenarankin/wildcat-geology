# aapg-density-neutron-log-porosity

**Covers:** An AAPG Treatise-derived explainer on computing porosity from density logs and interpreting density–neutron log pairs and crossplots (crossover, gas effect, lithology ambiguity).

**Authoritative for:** density porosity equation and choice of matrix density for sandstone, limestone, dolomite, anhydrite, and salt, selecting fluid density for freshwater vs saltwater (mud filtrate) conditions, explaining why density and neutron porosity readings diverge in a zone, recognizing density–neutron crossover and distinguishing gas from light or residual oil, why shales plot toward the dolomite line and why mixed mineralogy (e.g., dolomite-cemented sandstone) makes crossplots ambiguous

**Not good for:** tool physics, calibration, borehole/environmental corrections, or vendor-specific log presentation details, other porosity methods (sonic, NMR, core-measured porosity) or resistivity/saturation calculations, permeability, net-pay cutoffs, or full petrophysical workflows and uncertainty quantification, stratigraphic, structural, or basin-specific interpretation; no field case studies or regional datasets, unconventional/organic-rich shale petrophysics where matrix and kerogen effects dominate

**Scope:** Generic, non-regional log-analysis reference material at textbook level; assumes conventional wireline density and neutron logs run in typical clastic/carbonate/evaporite lithologies with standard matrix and fluid assumptions. Values quoted are conventional defaults, not substitutes for local core or tool calibration, and the treatment predates modern multi-mineral and NMR-based inversion practice.
