# slb-defining-series-log-interpretation

**Covers:** An introductory explainer on reading and interpreting common open-hole well logs (resistivity, SP, gamma ray, neutron, density, sonic), from Schlumberger's Defining Series educational material.

**Authoritative for:** what each standard log curve physically measures, direction and meaning of SP deflection across permeable beds, why shales read high on gamma ray, difference between shallow, medium and deep resistivity curves and invasion profiles, recognizing candidate pay zones from curve deflections, neutron-density crossover and the gas effect, basic conversion of bulk density and sonic transit time to porosity, qualitative link between hydrocarbons and elevated resistivity

**Not good for:** field- or basin-specific interpretation parameters (matrix densities, Rw, a/m/n exponents), quantitative reserves or saturation calculations requiring calibrated inputs, tool specifications, calibration procedures, or vendor tool-string details, cased-hole, production, or image/NMR log interpretation, stratigraphic correlation, facies analysis, or depositional interpretation, modern LWD, dielectric, or multi-frequency array processing, borehole environmental correction workflows in detail

**Scope:** Vendor-produced tutorial/primer level, generic and non-regional; conventional clastic and carbonate open-hole logging concepts presented qualitatively with simple textbook equations. Use it to learn or sanity-check curve reading, not as a source for numerical parameters, local cutoffs, or tool-specific accuracy claims.
