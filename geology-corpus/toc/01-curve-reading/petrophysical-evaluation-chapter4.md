# petrophysical-evaluation-chapter4

**Covers:** Chapter 4 of a petrophysical evaluation study: a worked deterministic log-analysis workflow for the Sharaf-1 well using Interactive Petrophysics (IP), from curve inventory through shale volume, porosity, Rw, water saturation, permeability, fluid contacts and pay cutoffs.

**Authoritative for:** Step-by-step deterministic petrophysical workflow in Interactive Petrophysics (module sequence, inputs required), Linear gamma-ray shale volume method, including the specific GRclean/GRclay endpoints picked per zone in Sharaf-1, Neutron-density crossplot as a clay indicator and the double clay indicator equation, Rw determination from Rmf/Rt/Rxo and from a Pickett plot, Indonesian (Poupon-Leveaux) water saturation equation for shaly sands, with the a, m, n values used here, Timur permeability estimation from porosity and irreducible water saturation, Formation temperature correction, fluid contact picking and net-pay cutoff selection as applied in this well, Log suite availability and logged depth range for Sharaf-1; interval with oil and gas shows

**Not good for:** Regional geology, stratigraphy, structure or basin history — this is a log-analysis chapter, not a geological synthesis, Core analysis, SCAL, capillary pressure or NMR interpretation, Probabilistic/multi-mineral solver workflows, or non-IP software procedures, Carbonate, fractured or unconventional reservoir evaluation (models here are shaly-sand oriented), Transferring the specific GRclean/GRclay picks, Rw, a/m/n, or cutoff values to any other well or field — these are well-specific calibrations, Reserves estimation, volumetrics or economics beyond the pay flagging step, Tool physics, borehole environmental corrections, or log QC theory

**Scope:** Single-well case study (Sharaf-1) using conventional wireline curves and a deterministic shaly-sand model in Interactive Petrophysics; all parameter picks are local calibrations. Methods (linear Vsh, Indonesian Sw, Timur k) are standard industry equations and are portable, but the numbers are not. No date or basin context is given in the section entries beyond the well name, so treat regional applicability as unestablished.
