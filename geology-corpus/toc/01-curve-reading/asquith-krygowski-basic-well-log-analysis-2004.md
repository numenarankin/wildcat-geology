# asquith-krygowski-basic-well-log-analysis-2004

**Covers:** A practical petrophysics textbook (Asquith & Krygowski, AAPG Methods in Exploration 16, 2004 revised edition) teaching open-hole well log interpretation for geologists, from curve reading through porosity, resistivity, NMR, saturation crossplots, shaly-sand analysis, lithology mapping, and borehole imaging.

**Authoritative for:** definitions of borehole-environment symbols (Rm, Rmf, Rmc, Rw, Rxo, Ri, Rt) and step/transition/annulus invasion profiles, Archie equation practice, including default a/m/n choices when no local data exist, Rw determination from the SP log (SSP, Rwe-to-Rw conversion, bed-thickness correction), Vshale from gamma ray: gamma ray index plus Larionov (Tertiary and older), Steiber and Clavier nonlinear equations, sonic porosity by Wyllie time-average and Raymer-Hunt-Gardner, compaction factor Cp, secondary porosity index, density porosity from RHOB with matrix densities for sandstone, limestone, dolomite, anhydrite, salt; DRHO quality check, neutron log gas and shale effects and service-company chart dependence, neutron-density quick-look lithology, crossover interpretation, and porosity crossplots, M-N plots, MID plots, rho-maa / Dt-maa / Umaa apparent matrix calculations and mineral tables, induction vs laterolog tool selection, dual induction and dual laterolog-MSFL tornado chart invasion corrections (Rint-2c, Rint-9b), Rt-min, microlog permeability indication (positive/negative separation) and microresistivity flushed-zone tools, NMR fundamentals: T2 distributions, T2 cutoffs, BVI/FFI/MPHI, Coates and SDR permeability, TDA and diffusion hydrocarbon typing, quick-look saturation methods: Rwa, ratio method, moveable hydrocarbon index, Ro overlay, bulk volume water/Buckles, Pickett and Hingle crossplot construction and reading (Rw, m, matrix values, Sw), log-derived permeability estimates: Timur, Wyllie-Rose, Coates-Dumanoir, shaly-sand corrections and Sw equations (Simandoux, Fertl, dispersed-clay q), SP alpha mapping and gamma-ray API cutoffs from Vshale cutoffs for facies/net-pay mapping, electrical and acoustic borehole image tool physics, coverage, processing, and interpretation basics

**Not good for:** cased-hole logging, production logging, cement evaluation, or perforation/completion evaluation, modern probabilistic/inversion multimineral solvers, machine-learning log prediction, or post-2004 tool generations, unconventional shale-reservoir petrophysics (TOC, kerogen, geomechanics, sourced-rock logs), formation testing, pressure/DST analysis, mud logging, or core analysis procedures, capillary pressure, SCAL, or reservoir simulation inputs beyond simple log-derived Sw and k, seismic, stratigraphic correlation theory, or basin analysis beyond simple log-based mapping examples, authoritative regional stratigraphy or type-log tie-ins outside its illustrative examples, current service-company chartbooks - tool-specific charts must come from the vendor and current tool version

**Scope:** North American practice-oriented, pre-2005 tool vintage; examples lean on U.S. wells (Gulf Coast, Rocky Mountain, Permian/Fusselman carbonates) with English units first and metric secondary. Open hole only. Deterministic, chart-and-equation workflows assume Archie-type clean reservoirs with shaly-sand corrections as add-ons; service-company chart references (Schlumberger, Halliburton) are for tools of that era and are not interchangeable between vendors. Uses shale volume rather than clay volume throughout, so it does not align with clay-mineral-based CEC models.
