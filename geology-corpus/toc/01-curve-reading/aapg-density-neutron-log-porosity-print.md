# aapg-density-neutron-log-porosity-print

**Covers:** An AAPG Wiki reference page explaining how to compute porosity from density and neutron logs, including matrix and fluid density values and how to interpret density-neutron curve separation and crossover.

**Authoritative for:** the density porosity equation (φ = (ρma − ρb)/(ρma − ρf)) and its inputs, standard matrix density values for sandstone, limestone, dolomite, anhydrite and salt, choosing fluid density for freshwater vs saltwater mud filtrate, qualitative interpretation of density-neutron separation and crossover (gas effect, shale effect, lithology mismatch)

**Not good for:** shale-volume, saturation, or permeability calculations, tool physics, calibration, or vendor-specific logging hardware details, core-calibrated or basin-specific porosity cutoffs and net-reservoir criteria, acoustic/sonic, NMR, or resistivity-based porosity methods, depositional or stratigraphic interpretation

**Scope:** Generic petrophysical tutorial content with no geographic or stratigraphic tie; matrix and fluid density constants are conventional textbook values and should be replaced with local core or mud-filtrate measurements for quantitative work.
