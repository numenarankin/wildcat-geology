# hapsari-dynamic-simulation-liquid-loading-horizontal

**Covers:** A case-study paper applying OLGA transient/dynamic multiphase simulation to liquid loading in a horizontal gas well (well X-1, X Field, Indonesia), benchmarked against the steady-state Turner and Coleman critical-gas-rate correlations, with a velocity-string vs bullheading de-liquification comparison.

**Authoritative for:** the Turner droplet terminal-velocity equation and Coleman's 20%-reduced variant, as written formulas, why steady-state critical-rate correlations underpredict in horizontal/deviated wells and large-diameter tubing, using dynamic (OLGA-type) transient simulation to estimate critical gas rate and liquid holdup/accumulation in a wellbore, worked comparison of 3-inch vs 4-inch velocity string critical rates and liquid accumulation for one well, the reasoning and economics behind choosing a velocity string over bullheading in this case

**Not good for:** reservoir characterization, stratigraphy, or geology of X Field (production-engineering paper, field is anonymized), generalized correlation coefficients validated across many wells or basins, vertical-well liquid loading where plain Turner/Coleman already suffices, other de-liquification methods in depth (gas lift, plunger lift, ESP, foam, compression), OLGA software setup, tuning, or flow-model validation details sufficient to reproduce the run, field-wide or regional Indonesian gas production statistics

**Scope:** Single-well, single-field case study (one horizontal gas well, X Field Indonesia, anonymized); modern-era production/flow-assurance engineering, not geology; conclusions are specific to that well's geometry, fluids and tubing sizes and should not be extrapolated to other wells without re-simulation.
