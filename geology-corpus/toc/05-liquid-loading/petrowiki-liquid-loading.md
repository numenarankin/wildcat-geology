# petrowiki-liquid-loading

**Covers:** A PetroWiki encyclopedia page on liquid loading in gas wells: definition, causes, flow-pattern evolution, and the two standard onset criteria (Turner/Coleman droplet model and Barnea film-reversal model) plus symptoms and remediation.

**Authoritative for:** definition and physical mechanism of liquid loading in gas wells, Turner et al. critical gas velocity / terminal droplet velocity equations and the 20% upward adjustment, Coleman et al. modification of the Turner criterion for low-pressure wells, Barnea film reversal model and interfacial shear stress vs. liquid film thickness, effect of tubing diameter, reservoir pressure decline, and gas velocity on loading risk, recognizing symptoms of liquid loading and general remediation options

**Not good for:** field-specific or basin-specific case histories and production data, detailed design or sizing of deliquification hardware (plunger lift, velocity strings, foamers, compression) beyond mention, multiphase flow correlations for oil wells or general pressure-drop calculation, reservoir engineering, deliverability forecasting, or nodal analysis workflows, horizontal/deviated well loading behavior and modern mechanistic transient models, peer-reviewed derivations, uncertainty ranges, or benchmarking of the models against large datasets

**Scope:** Generic, non-geographic petroleum-engineering reference content (wiki-style, derived from SPE handbook material); vertical gas well production tubing focus; models presented are the classic 1969-1991 era droplet and film-reversal criteria, so treat as screening-level rules of thumb rather than current best-practice numerics.
