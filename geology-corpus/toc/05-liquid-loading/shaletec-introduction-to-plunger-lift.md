# shaletec-introduction-to-plunger-lift

**Covers:** A vendor-style technical primer (ShaleTec) on plunger lift artificial lift: the operating cycle, well/candidate requirements, applications, benefits, limitations, economics, and basic design equations for liquid-loaded gas wells.

**Authoritative for:** stages of a conventional plunger lift cycle and what happens in each, target plunger rise velocity guidelines, conventional versus continuous-run plunger operating differences, candidate screening criteria for plunger lift (GLR, tubing ID, deviation, sand tolerance), rule-of-thumb gas volume required per barrel of liquid per 1000 ft of lift, with and without a packer, expected production uplift and economic rationale for installing plunger lift

**Not good for:** other artificial lift methods (rod pump, gas lift, ESP, jet pump) beyond passing comparison, reservoir engineering, decline analysis, or reserves estimation, liquid-loading physics derivations such as Turner or Coleman critical-velocity modeling, well-specific equipment sizing, controller programming, or vendor hardware specifications, stratigraphy, geology, or formation evaluation of any basin, field case histories with measured data

**Scope:** Modern (shale-era) production-engineering practice guidance, written at introductory/marketing-technical level; rules of thumb and generalized equations rather than peer-reviewed derivations. Basin-agnostic but framed around unconventional gas wells with low rates and liquid loading. Treat numeric thresholds as screening heuristics, not design authority, and validate against operator standards and current literature.
