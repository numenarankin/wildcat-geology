# conglomerate-reservoir-lithology-identification-ml

**Covers:** A machine-learning study applying boosting ensembles (AdaBoost, LightGBM, XGBoost) to conventional well logs to classify lithology in fan-delta sandstone-conglomerate reservoirs, with grid-search tuning, confusion-matrix/ROC metrics, and SHAP interpretation.

**Authoritative for:** why cross-plot and GR-cutoff lithology discrimination breaks down in gravel-bearing conglomerate reservoirs (high GR in gravelly sandstone), selecting and pruning log-curve inputs (GR, DT, RHOB, TNPH, resistivity) for ML lithology models, including dropping collinear resistivity curves such as M2R6/M2RX in favor of M2R1, boosting model construction and grid-search hyperparameter ranges (learning_rate, max_depth, n_estimators) for log-based lithology classification in scikit-learn/LightGBM/XGBoost, evaluating multiclass lithology classifiers: confusion matrix, precision/recall/F1, accuracy, ROC/AUC interpretation, SHAP-based feature-importance ranking of log curves for distinguishing mudstone, sandstone, and conglomerate classes, core-to-log labeling workflow and train/test splitting of core-calibrated log datasets

**Not good for:** conventional deterministic or probabilistic petrophysical evaluation (porosity, permeability, saturation calculation), depositional-model, sequence-stratigraphic, or diagenetic interpretation of conglomerate systems, reservoir quality prediction, productivity forecasting, or completion design, image-log, NMR, or advanced-tool-based lithofacies analysis, transferable model coefficients or hyperparameters for other basins or tool suites without retraining, non-conglomerate carbonate, shale, or deepwater clastic lithology classification

**Scope:** Single-study, data-driven work on a fan-delta conglomerate reservoir using one operator's conventional wireline suite and core-defined lithology labels; accuracies, optimal hyperparameters, and SHAP rankings are specific to that dataset and log-tool naming (M2R-series induction curves) and should be treated as a methodology template rather than universal calibration. Modern ML-era study; assumes familiarity with Python ML tooling.
