# ahmed-reservoir-engineering-handbook

**Covers:** Textbook/handbook chapters from Ahmed's *Reservoir Engineering Handbook* covering reservoir fluid classification, gas and crude oil PVT property correlations, laboratory PVT analysis (CCE/DL/separator/CVD), and basic rock properties (porosity, saturation, capillary pressure, permeability).

**Authoritative for:** pressure-temperature phase diagram interpretation and classification of oil/gas reservoirs (undersaturated, saturated, gas-cap, retrograde condensate, near-critical, wet gas, dry gas), GOR, API gravity, and condensate-gravity rules of thumb for identifying crude oil and gas reservoir type, gas z-factor methods: Standing-Katz chart basis, Hall-Yarborough, Dranchuk-Abu-Kassem, Dranchuk-Purvis-Robinson, with constants and valid pr/Tr ranges, pseudo-critical property calculation and corrections: Kay's rules, Stewart-Burkhardt-Voo (J,K), Wichert-Aziz sour gas, Carr-Kobayashi-Burrows nonhydrocarbon, Sutton high-MW gas, gas density, apparent molecular weight, gas FVF (Bg), expansion factor (Eg), and isothermal gas compressibility equations, gas viscosity correlations (Carr-Kobayashi-Burrows/Dempsey, Lee-Gonzalez-Eakin) including nonhydrocarbon corrections, black-oil correlations for Rs, bubble-point pressure, Bo, oil density, oil compressibility, and oil viscosity (Standing, Vasquez-Beggs, Glaso, Marhoun, Petrosky-Farshad, McCain, Chew-Connally, Beggs-Robinson) with coefficients and relative accuracy, characterizing undefined C7+ and petroleum fractions (Riazi-Daubert, Edmister acentric factor, Ahmed 1985 generalized Katz-Firoozabadi equation) and pure-component physical property tables, interpreting and reducing lab PVT reports: CCE relative volume and Y-function smoothing, differential liberation, separator tests and optimum separator pressure, Dodson-type conversion of DL data to flash/separator conditions, extrapolation to a new bubble point, gas-condensate lab work: separator recombination, GPM plant products, constant-volume depletion, two-phase z-factor, retrograde liquid dropout to oil saturation, porosity/pore volume/saturation definitions including the 7,758 Ahφ volumetric formula and permeability/porosity averaging, wettability, contact angle, interfacial tension, capillary pressure measurement (restored state), hysteresis, Leverett J-function, free water level and transition-zone calculations, Darcy's law in linear and radial form, Klinkenberg gas-slippage correction, and arithmetic/harmonic/geometric permeability averaging

**Not good for:** field-specific or basin-specific reservoir descriptions, stratigraphy, sedimentology, or structural geology, well log interpretation and petrophysical log analysis workflows (no resistivity/porosity log methods here), compositional EOS modeling, flash calculations, and phase-behavior simulation detail beyond correlation-level characterization, well test analysis, decline curve analysis, material balance, waterflooding, or numerical simulation (covered in other chapters not represented in this section), unconventional/shale reservoirs, hydraulic fracturing, geomechanics, or sorbed-gas storage, drilling, completion, or surface facility design, authoritative accuracy for heavy oils, volatile/near-critical fluids, or exotic gas compositions where the cited empirical correlations were not calibrated

**Scope:** General-purpose petroleum engineering textbook (1st-edition preface era, correlations mostly 1940s-1990s vintage; drawn largely from US Gulf Coast, California, and Middle East crude/gas datasets). Equations are in oilfield units (psia, °R, bbl, scf, mD) and empirical correlations carry stated API, GOR, temperature, and pseudo-reduced pressure limits that must be checked before extrapolating. Worked examples (Big Butte, Mtech field) are illustrative teaching cases, not field studies. Trust it for method, coefficients, and units; not for modern published correlation updates, machine-learning PVT models, or region-specific tuning.
