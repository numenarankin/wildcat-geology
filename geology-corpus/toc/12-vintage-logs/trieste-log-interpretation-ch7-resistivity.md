# trieste-log-interpretation-ch7-resistivity

**Covers:** Chapter 7 of a Schlumberger-era well log interpretation text (Trieste course notes), covering resistivity logging tools, their responses, and correction charts from vintage ES logs through laterologs, induction/Phasor induction, microresistivity devices, and LWD CDR.

**Authoritative for:** identifying and reading old electrical survey (ES) logs: 16-in and 64-in normals, lateral curve asymmetry, blind zones, spurious peaks, and rules of thumb for estimating Rt from vintage curves, laterolog family tool physics and selection: LL3, LL7, LL8, dual laterolog with MicroSFL, Delaware and Groningen effects, SFL, induction logging principles and hardware generations: 6FF40, DIL-LL8, ISF, DIL-SFL, Phasor Induction SFL, X-signal/quadrature processing, shoulder-effect correction, IDPH/IMPH/IDER/IMER/IDVR/IMVR mnemonics and their bed-thickness requirements, choosing induction vs laterolog from Rmf/Rw and mud type (salt mud, fresh mud, oil-base mud, air-drilled holes), microresistivity Rxo tools — microlog, microlaterolog, Proximity, MicroSFL — including positive separation as a permeability indicator and mudcake correction limits, using tornado (Rint) charts to solve for Rt, Rxo and invasion diameter di from three resistivity curves, including oil-based-mud variants, Compensated Dual Resistivity (CDR) LWD phase-shift vs attenuation resistivity, depths of investigation, and Rad/Rps crossover for bed boundaries and invasion

**Not good for:** modern array-induction, triaxial/anisotropy, or deep-reading azimuthal LWD resistivity tools postdating the Phasor generation, porosity, density, neutron, sonic, NMR, dielectric or imaging logs (other chapters), saturation equations, Archie parameters, shaly-sand models or volumetric petrophysical workflows beyond Rt/Rxo extraction, non-Schlumberger tool mnemonics, specs and service-company equivalents, digital log processing, inversion software, or depth-matching workflows, regional stratigraphy, formation names, or field-specific interpretation examples

**Scope:** Vendor-specific (Schlumberger) training material of roughly late-1980s to early-1990s vintage; tool specs, chart names (Rint-9, mudcake corrections) and resistivity ranges are tied to that toolset and those printed chart books. Chart-based hand corrections assume vertical holes, circular boreholes, step-profile invasion and isotropic beds; conclusions about thin-bed resolution reflect pre-array processing limits. Vintage-ES guidance is valuable for legacy wells worldwide but the underlying physics discussion is generic, not basin-specific.
