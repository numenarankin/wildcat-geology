# epa-environmental-geophysics-electrical-methods

**Covers:** An EPA environmental-geophysics manual chapter on electrical methods, indexed here through its vintage borehole-logging material: SP and single-point resistance, normal/lateral and focused resistivity, microresistivity, dipmeter, and induction tools, with acquisition, correction, and Rw/Rt interpretation workflows.

**Authoritative for:** reading and interpreting legacy analog electric logs (SP, single-point resistance, 16-in and 64-in normals, laterals) common in water-well and older environmental files, calculating Rw from SP deflection or from long-normal resistivity via formation factor, including the assumptions that break down in fresh groundwater, standard AM/AO electrode spacings, depth and volume of investigation for normal, lateral, guard/laterolog and microresistivity devices, applying borehole-diameter, mud-resistivity, bed-thickness and invasion corrections to non-focused and focused resistivity logs, diagnosing anomalous SP behavior (positive deflection, flat or zero curve) and picking lithologic contacts on SP, choosing between focused and non-focused microresistivity devices; calibrating focused tools when the geometric factor is unknown, screening-level use of electrical logs for groundwater salinity, aquifer/confining-bed identification, and contamination assessment

**Not good for:** modern digital petrophysics: array induction, LWD, NMR, dielectric, or triaxial induction processing, quantitative hydrocarbon saturation, net-pay, or reserves calculations in oil and gas reservoirs, nuclear and acoustic logs (gamma, neutron, density, sonic) and multi-mineral log analysis, surface geophysical methods beyond the electrical family, or seismic/gravity/magnetics, carbonate, fractured, or shaly-sand-specific saturation models and clay-corrected resistivity equations, regulatory compliance requirements, sampling protocols, or lab water-chemistry procedures, deep, high-temperature, or high-pressure wellbore conditions and modern tool specifications

**Scope:** United States EPA guidance aimed at shallow environmental and groundwater investigations, largely fresh to brackish water, unconsolidated to shallow consolidated sediments, small-diameter mud- or water-filled boreholes. The logging content documents mid-20th-century tool generations and chart-based hand corrections; equations, spacings, and correction charts are tied to those vintage tools and should not be substituted for current service-company response models or vendor charts. Indexed sections here cover only the vintage-log portion, so other parts of the electrical-methods manual (e.g. surface resistivity, IP, EM) may be present but are not represented in this card.
