# aapg-perforation-design

**Covers:** An AAPG-style primer chapter on well perforation design, covering perforating hardware, conveyance methods, shaped-charge mechanics, and the design factors that control flow efficiency of a perforated interval.

**Authoritative for:** definitions and purpose of perforating in cased-hole completions, conveyance options: casing guns, through-tubing guns, tubing-conveyed perforating (TCP) and when each is selected, hollow carrier vs exposed (strip/capsule) gun construction and trade-offs, anatomy and function of a shaped charge (case, explosive, liner, primer), gun classification and basic charge performance terminology, underbalanced vs overbalanced perforating rationale, qualitative relationships between perforation length, phasing, density, crushed-zone damage, skin and productivity

**Not good for:** quantitative perforation-skin or inflow-performance calculations for a specific well, modern API RP 19B charge performance data or vendor charge selection, open-hole, slotted-liner, or sand-control completion design, hydraulic fracture initiation and limited-entry frac perforating design, explosives handling regulations, HSE procedures, or wellsite operating permits, perforating in unconventional horizontal multistage plug-and-perf programs, stratigraphy, basin geology, reservoir characterization, or log interpretation

**Scope:** Engineering/completions handbook material, not regional geology; conceptual and generic rather than basin- or field-specific, and reflecting AAPG-methods-era practice, so hardware specifications, charge performance standards and shale-era perforating strategies may be dated relative to current service-company technology.
