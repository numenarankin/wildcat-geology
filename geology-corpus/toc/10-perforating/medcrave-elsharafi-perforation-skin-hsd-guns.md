# medcrave-elsharafi-perforation-skin-hsd-guns

**Covers:** A short applied petroleum-engineering paper (MedCrave) evaluating HSD perforating gun designs in four Hungarian (MOL) vertical oil wells by computing composite perforation skin with the Karakas & Tariq method and predicting oil flow rates.

**Authoritative for:** step-by-step Karakas & Tariq perforation skin calculation, including alpha-theta values, a1/a2/b1/b2 vertical skin coefficients and C1/C2 wellbore-skin constants for 0, 180, 120, 90, 60 and 45 degree phasing, worked example of perforation penetration depth from formation compressive strength (Thompson correlation) using an 1.1 psi/ft overburden gradient and reservoir pressure, decomposition of total skin in a perforated well into crushed-zone, perforation (plane-flow, vertical, wellbore) and damage-zone components, estimating post-perforation productivity/flow rate before perforating for a given gun, shot density and phasing, comparison of HSD gun types and shot densities against resulting skin factor and oil flow rate in the four case wells, reservoir input data (pressure, permeability, porosity, viscosity, perforation geometry) for the four Hungarian study wells

**Not good for:** horizontal, deviated or multi-stage fractured completions, gas, gas-condensate or high-rate turbulent (non-Darcy) perforation flow analysis, perforating gun hardware engineering, explosive/charge design or API RP-19B test data, underbalance/dynamic-underbalance perforating design, propellant or extreme-overbalance stimulation, perforation cleanup, debris, or crushed-zone permeability measurement (values here are assumed, not measured), field-wide reservoir characterization, stratigraphy or basin geology of Hungary, transient pressure test interpretation or independent skin measurement from well tests, sand control, gravel-packed or frac-pack completion skin

**Scope:** Modern (post-2015) applied case study limited to four vertical, cased-and-perforated oil wells in Hungary operated by MOL; results depend on assumed damaged-zone, crushed-zone and kv/kh values where perforation data were unavailable, so numbers are illustrative rather than measured. Methodology is generic analytical steady-state single-phase Darcy skin theory and transfers anywhere, but the well-specific skins, flow rates and gun rankings do not.
