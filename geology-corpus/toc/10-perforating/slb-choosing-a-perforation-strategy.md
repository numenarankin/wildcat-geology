# slb-choosing-a-perforation-strategy

**Covers:** A Schlumberger completions-engineering chapter on choosing a perforation strategy: gun and charge types, conveyance methods, shot density/phasing/hole-size selection by completion type, underbalance practice, and firing-system safety.

**Authoritative for:** selecting perforating gun type and conveyance (through-tubing, through-casing, tubing-conveyed; exposed vs hollow carrier), matching perforation geometry (shot density, phasing, entrance hole diameter, penetration depth) to natural, stimulated, or gravel-pack completions, underbalance perforating practice, cleanup criteria, and wireline underbalance limits, limited-entry perforating, ball sealers, and avoiding proppant bridging (perforation-to-proppant diameter ratios), big-hole perforating for sand control and gravel packs, and gun/casing standoff effects on hole size, perforation-related skin, crushed zone, and phasing considerations in naturally fractured or anisotropic/laminated reservoirs, perforating charge and gun terminology (primary/secondary explosives, detonating cord, big-hole vs deep-penetrating charges), gun hostile-environment ratings (H2S, CO2, temperature/pressure) and wellsite arming/safety procedures including S.A.F.E. systems, monobore and dual-string high-shot-density gun deployment and long-interval perforating

**Not good for:** reservoir characterization, stratigraphy, basin history, or any regional geology, quantitative fracture design, acid job design, or gravel-pack pumping schedules beyond perforation requirements, log interpretation, petrophysical calculation, or formation evaluation workflows, current gun/charge model numbers, API 19B performance data, or up-to-date pressure/temperature ratings, cased-hole cement evaluation, casing design, or drilling engineering, non-explosive perforating alternatives, abrasive jetting, or modern plug-and-perf / multistage frac sleeve practice in unconventionals

**Scope:** Vendor-authored completions reference (Schlumberger), generic and worldwide in application rather than tied to any basin or field; reflects the tool catalog and best practice of its publication era, so specific gun systems, ratings and service names are dated and should be checked against current vendor specifications. Guidance is qualitative/rule-of-thumb rather than a design calculation manual.

_12 segments._
