# slb-new-dynamics-of-underbalanced-perforating

**Covers:** A Schlumberger Oilfield Review-style technical article on dynamic underbalance perforating, presenting the PURE process: its physics, lab transient-pressure testing, design/candidate selection, and field case histories.

**Authoritative for:** how dynamic underbalance (transient post-detonation pressure surge) cleans perforation tunnels versus static underbalance, crushed-zone damage from shaped charges and its effect on perforation skin and productivity ratio (Kc/K), history and critique of static underbalance design charts and criteria, single-shot and perforate-and-flow lab test results: core flow efficiency (CFE) and perforation skin for underbalanced, overbalanced, and dynamic-underbalance conditions, PURE gun-string and hardware configuration (TCP strings, PURE chambers, coiled tubing/CIRP, DST with IRDV tester valve, production valves), candidate screening, pressure/permeability limits, and shot-density vs perforation-length tradeoffs for PURE, documented field cases: East Painter/Nugget sandstone (ChevronTexaco), Borgsweer 4 and NAM Rotliegend, Brady field Weber formation, North Sea Rotliegend, CNR Ninian North N-41/N-42, claims about reducing or eliminating acid stimulation and perforation washes after perforating

**Not good for:** vendor-neutral comparison of competing dynamic-underbalance systems or generic perforating hardware selection, reservoir characterization, stratigraphy, or basin geology of the named fields beyond what the case studies mention, hydraulic fracturing design, acidizing chemistry, or completion design outside the perforating interval, standard static-underbalance design charts for routine jobs (the document argues these are conservative but is not a design manual), quantitative well-test interpretation, log analysis, or petrophysical property determination, sand control, gravel packing, or cased-hole mechanics beyond gun-sticking and sand-influx risk

**Scope:** Service-company promotional/technical literature (Schlumberger, PURE branded process and software), circa mid-2000s. Case studies are mostly tight/low-permeability sandstone gas and oil wells in the Rocky Mountains (Nugget, Weber), the Netherlands (Rotliegend, NAM), and the UK North Sea (Ninian). Findings and quoted CFE/skin values reflect Schlumberger PERF lab tests and its own field results; treat performance claims and the implied inadequacy of older static criteria as advocacy requiring independent corroboration, and verify current tool names, software, and pressure/permeability limits against modern sources.
