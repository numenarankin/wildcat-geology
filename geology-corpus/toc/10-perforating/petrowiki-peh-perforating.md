# petrowiki-peh-perforating

**Covers:** A PetroWiki Petroleum Engineering Handbook chapter on well perforating, covering its history, shaped-charge and alternative methods, and core terminology (explosives, phasing, shot density, flow efficiency).

**Authoritative for:** definitions of perforating terms: high vs low explosives, gas generators, high-order/low-order detonation, primary vs secondary explosives, phasing, shot density, history of bullet-gun and shaped-charge perforating adoption in the oil industry, non-explosive alternatives such as abrasive/hydraulic jet perforating, conceptual overview of the perforation flow path and what constitutes good perforation flow efficiency

**Not good for:** quantitative charge/gun selection design, penetration tables, or vendor-specific hardware specs, completion, fracturing, or sand-control design beyond the perforation itself, well-log or petrophysical calculations, reservoir characterization, or formation stratigraphy, field- or basin-specific case histories, pricing, or current HSE/regulatory requirements for explosives handling, detailed perforating job procedures, underbalance/overbalance surge modeling, or damage-removal quantification

**Scope:** General industry-wide engineering reference, not tied to any basin or formation; a handbook-style encyclopedic summary whose content reflects late-20th-century to early-2000s practice, so treat gun performance claims and standards as dated and verify against current API/service-company data.
