# fundamentals-of-well-perforating-manual

**Covers:** A training-manual chapter on fundamentals of well perforating: deployment methods, explosives and shaped charges, API RP 19B testing, perforation design and pressure conditions, and job workflow.

**Authoritative for:** choosing between wireline, tubing-conveyed, coiled-tubing and through-tubing perforating deployment, perforating explosive selection by temperature rating and detonation velocity (RDX, HMX, HNS, PETN, HTX, lead azide), API RP 19B charge test procedure, shot counts, aging, test fluids and reporting conventions, interpreting vendor charge performance tables (penetration, entrance hole diameter, shot density, phasing, area open to flow), overbalance / static underbalance / dynamic underbalance / extreme overbalance perforating strategy, perforation design criteria for water injectors (entry hole diameter, shot density), perforating job workflow from shop preparation to post-firing, including GR/CCL depth control, how rock mechanical properties, pore pressure and reservoir temperature affect perforation geometry

**Not good for:** reservoir geology, stratigraphy, facies or basin analysis, log interpretation beyond GR/CCL depth correlation; no petrophysical saturation or porosity workflows, hydraulic fracture design, acidizing or stimulation modelling detail, cased-hole cement evaluation, casing design or well integrity engineering, sand control, gravel pack or screen completion design, production/nodal analysis or inflow performance calculation, regional case histories or field-specific completion outcomes, current commercial charge catalogues (tables reflect the manual's vintage Schlumberger PowerJet/UltraJet/PowerFlow lines)

**Scope:** Vendor-oriented service-company training material, generic and worldwide in application rather than tied to any basin or play; charge specifications and temperature ratings are snapshots of a particular product generation and API RP 19B edition, so verify current vendor data sheets and standard revisions before design work. Engineering/completions domain only — contains no geological or stratigraphic content.

_6 segments._
