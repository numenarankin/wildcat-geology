# Water coning in vertical wells

## Critical rate concepts

Coning is the upward movement of water toward a producing interval when the
viscous drawdown at the wellbore exceeds the gravity forces holding the contact
down. Below the critical rate the cone is stable; above it, water breaks
through and the well makes water for the rest of its life.

Critical rate rises with the standoff between the perforations and the original
water contact, and falls as vertical permeability rises. A well with kv/kh near
unity cones at a much lower rate than a layered one.

## Correlations and their disagreement

Craft & Hawkins, Chaperon, Schols and Hoyland all give closed-form critical rate
expressions, and they do not agree with one another. Reporting one of them as
the critical rate overstates what is known. The defensible output is the spread
across the published correlations, with the assumption set stated.
