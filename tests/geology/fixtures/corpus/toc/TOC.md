## 04-coning

### coning-basics
- **Covers:** A short primer on water coning in vertical wells: what critical rate means and why the published correlations disagree.
- **Authoritative for:** what critical rate means physically, how standoff and kv/kh change critical rate, why coning correlations disagree and what to report instead

- **Scope:** Vertical wells, homogeneous or lightly layered. Closed-form correlations only.
